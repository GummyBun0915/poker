# Design System

## Scene

Five friends play late at night from their own screens, gathered around a walnut table under the warm pool of a listening-club lamp. The room is intimate and quiet; the cards, chips, and current decision carry all attention.

## Visual direction

Committed oxblood felt, espresso walnut, parchment cards, oxidized metal, and a restrained cobalt interaction signal. Avoid casino gold, green felt, neon esports styling, glass panels, and generic dashboard cards.

## Typography

Use Geist for interface text and a restrained serif only for the wordmark and card ranks. Numeric stacks use tabular figures. Labels are short, calm, and sentence case.

## Materials

- Felt: low-contrast woven noise and radial wear.
- Walnut: layered grain and warm edge highlights.
- Cards: warm stock, fine border, crisp suit symbols, realistic contact shadow.
- Chips: clay edge marks, denomination rings, compact stack shadows.

## Motion

Buttons respond in 120–160ms. State changes use 180–240ms exponential ease-out. Dealing, pot movement, and showdowns may take 300–650ms because they explain spatial state. Animate transforms and opacity; pause effects while the tab is hidden; honor reduced motion.

## Layout

The table fills the viewport. The local seat anchors to the bottom and the action tray sits directly below it. Remote seats follow the rail. Secondary history and chat use a collapsible side drawer on wide screens and a bottom sheet on phones.
