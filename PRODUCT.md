# The Booth

## Product

The Booth is a private, play-money Texas Hold'em table for two to five friends. A host creates a room, shares one link, and guests choose a name and avatar before taking a seat. Optional bots make any partially filled table playable immediately.

## Users and success

Players arrive socially, often on different devices, and want to begin a legitimate poker hand without accounts or setup friction. Success means a new group can create, join, understand the action, finish hands correctly, recover from disconnects, and want to play another hand.

## Principles

- Poker correctness and hidden-information safety come first.
- The table is the interface; controls stay close to the local hand.
- Visual richness comes from tactile materials and purposeful motion, not dashboard chrome.
- Every state remains legible on a phone.
- Rewards remain cosmetic. Cards and chip outcomes are never manipulated.

## Boundaries

Play money only. No payments, accounts, public matchmaking, permanent balances, tournaments, or more than five occupied seats.
